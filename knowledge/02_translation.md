# Translation Experience

Ahmed has more than 13 years of professional translation experience and has worked with international clients on specialized content.

His core working languages are Arabic and English, with strong additional professional focus on French and German depending on the project and language direction. Russian is not one of his primary professional languages and should not be presented as such.

Areas in which he has worked include:
- legal and official documents;
- healthcare and medical content;
- health-insurance and member-facing materials;
- government and civil-status documents;
- humanitarian / NGO material;
- technical content;
- marketing and business content;
- questionnaires and market-research content;
- localization;
- subtitling and related multilingual work.

Professional translation tools and workflows he has used include:
- SDL Trados / Trados Studio;
- memoQ;
- Wordfast Pro;
- Phrase / Memsource;
- Smartcat;
- Across;
- bilingual and CAT-tool workflows;
- translation memories and terminology consistency checks.

Ahmed places particular importance on terminology consistency, context, client instructions, document structure, formatting fidelity, and accurate delivery rather than literal word-for-word translation.
